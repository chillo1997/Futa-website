A simpler way to request a payment

Here is how FutaPay is designed to work on supported routes.

1. Create a link

The receiver enters the amount and selects an available payout option.

2. Share it

Send the link through WhatsApp, SMS or email. The sender reviews the payment details and available payment methods.

3. Receive the payment

After payment confirmation and required checks, the payout is processed. Available methods and timing depend on the route.

Meet FutaPay

FutaPay is a product being developed by Nyota Technologies in the Netherlands. We are building a payment-link experience to help people connected to Europe and Africa request and receive payments more easily.

What happens next?

We are preparing partner integrations and testing the payment journey. We will announce confirmed routes, payment methods and launch pricing before opening the service.

What will a payment cost?

Launch pricing is being finalised. Fees and exchange rates will depend on the route and payment method.
